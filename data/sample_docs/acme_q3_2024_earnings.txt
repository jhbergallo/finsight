ACME CORPORATION
Q3 2024 EARNINGS REPORT
For the quarter ended September 30, 2024

---

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Acme Corporation delivered a strong third quarter, with revenue reaching $4.2 billion, representing 18% year-over-year growth. Net income came in at $610 million, up 22% compared to Q3 2023. Diluted earnings per share were $2.41, ahead of analyst consensus of $2.28.

Gross margin expanded 120 basis points to 54.3%, driven by improved operating leverage in our software segment and continued cost discipline across supply chain operations. Operating margin was 21.7%, compared to 19.4% in the prior year period.

---

SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

Software & Cloud Services
Revenue: $2.1 billion (+31% YoY)
Operating Income: $630 million
Operating Margin: 30%

Our cloud subscription business continues to be the primary growth driver. Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) reached $7.8 billion, up from $5.9 billion a year ago. Net Revenue Retention (NRR) held steady at 118%, reflecting strong expansion within our existing customer base. We added 1,240 net new enterprise customers during the quarter, bringing the total to 14,800 enterprise accounts.

Hardware & Devices
Revenue: $1.4 billion (+4% YoY)
Operating Income: $182 million
Operating Margin: 13%

Hardware revenue growth moderated as expected given the product cycle timing. Demand for our industrial sensor line remained resilient, particularly in the manufacturing vertical. We expect a refresh cycle in Q1 2025 to re-accelerate this segment.

Professional Services
Revenue: $700 million (+9% YoY)
Operating Income: $105 million
Operating Margin: 15%

Services revenue benefited from strong implementation demand tied to new software contracts signed in H1 2024. Backlog stands at $1.1 billion, providing good visibility into Q4 and early 2025.

RISK FACTORS

Macroeconomic Uncertainty
Prolonged high interest rates and softening enterprise IT budgets in certain geographies, particularly Europe, could weigh on new logo acquisition and deal sizes in the near term. We are monitoring pipeline conversion rates closely.

Foreign Exchange
Approximately 38% of revenue is denominated in currencies other than the US dollar. Currency headwinds reduced reported revenue growth by approximately 2 percentage points in Q3. We use natural hedging and forward contracts to partially mitigate this exposure.

Competition
The cloud and enterprise software markets remain intensely competitive. Several large technology companies have increased investment in overlapping product categories, which may create pricing pressure over time.

Customer Concentration
Our top 10 customers represent approximately 19% of total revenue. The loss of one or more of these relationships could have a material impact on results.

Cybersecurity
As a provider of enterprise software, we face ongoing and evolving cybersecurity threats. A material breach could harm our reputation and result in significant remediation costs and legal liability.

MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY

CEO Statement — Jane Holloway:

"Q3 was a quarter where execution really stood out. The team delivered strong results across the board while continuing to invest in our AI-native product roadmap. We are particularly encouraged by the momentum in our cloud segment and the quality of the deals we're signing — average contract values are up 14% year-over-year, which speaks to the value customers are getting from the platform.

Looking ahead, we remain confident in our full-year guidance. The pipeline going into Q4 is healthy, and early indicators from our new AI assistant product — launched in September — are very positive. We have over 400 enterprise pilots underway, and initial retention and engagement metrics are exceeding our internal benchmarks."

CFO Statement — David Park:

"Our financial model continues to demonstrate strong operating leverage. Gross margin expansion, combined with disciplined opex management, is translating revenue growth into even faster earnings growth. Free cash flow conversion remains above 90% of net income, which gives us significant flexibility to invest in the business and return capital to shareholders.

We are reiterating full-year revenue guidance of $16.4 to $16.6 billion and raising our EPS guidance to $9.20 to $9.35, up from the prior range of $8.95 to $9.15."
